# Paper Deep Reading

Use this skill for a supplied PDF, arXiv identifier, DOI, or stable publication page.

## Evidence discipline

- Read the abstract, methods, experiments, results, limitations, and relevant appendices before issuing a complete assessment.
- Distinguish what the authors report from your interpretation.
- Preserve page, section, figure, table, or theorem locators for consequential claims.
- Do not infer experimental results from an abstract alone.

## Note structure

1. Bibliographic record and one-sentence contribution.
2. Problem, assumptions, and why the problem matters.
3. Method: inputs, update rule or architecture, objective, and implementation-relevant details.
4. Evidence: datasets/environments, baselines, metrics, main results, ablations, and cost where reported.
5. Strengths, limitations, threats to validity, and reproducibility assets.
6. Relation to the active literature map and questions to pursue next.

For visual or mathematical papers, inspect key figures, tables, and equations rather than relying only on extracted text.
