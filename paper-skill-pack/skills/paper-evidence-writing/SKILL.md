# Paper Evidence Writing

Use this skill to outline, draft, revise, or audit a paper section after the relevant sources and results are available.

## Before drafting

Create a claim ledger. Each nontrivial factual claim needs an evidence ID, source locator, and confidence state. Candidate papers and model memory can inform search terms or prose, but cannot become citations until verified.

## Drafting rules

- State the research question, scope, and contribution before describing implementation details.
- Use calibrated language: report, finds, is associated with, or suggests when causality is not identified.
- Keep methods reproducible: data splits, exclusions, baselines, hyperparameters, evaluation protocol, and uncertainty reporting belong in the manuscript or appendix.
- Keep related work comparative: explain the nearest difference in task, assumption, method, data, or evaluation.
- Never convert a product feature list into a research contribution without empirical evidence.

## Final audit

Check every citation, numerical result, table/figure reference, novelty statement, and limitation claim against the ledger. Surface unsupported claims instead of filling gaps with plausible prose.
